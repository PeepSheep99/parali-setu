The smoke over North India starts in a paddy field. We buy the straw instead.

Farmers have about two weeks between the paddy harvest and sowing wheat. Burning the straw is fast and nearly free. Parali Setu makes not burning faster: book a pickup by talking in Punjabi or Hindi, we clear the field before you sow, and you are paid the same day. No vendors to chase, no machines to rent. How we earn: we buy at one posted price, store the straw, and sell it all year to industry at cost plus a 10% margin.

How it works

One booking for the farmer. One supplier for industry.

Farmer

  1. Talk once. “Das kille jhona, Sangrur, 25 tak kanak bijni hai.” Our Gemini agent asks only what's missing and reads it back.
  2. One fair price. Today's posted price for your district, set by our planner. No bargaining.
  3. A slot before sowing. Our crew, or a hired partner crew, clears the field for free.
  4. Paid the same day the straw is collected.

Parali Setu

  1. Forecast the harvest. 25 seasons of satellite fire data, forecast with TimesFM in BigQuery, show when each district's straw comes.
  2. Plan what to buy. A linear program weighs expected straw, depot space, crews and customer orders, and sets each district's price.
  3. Route the crews. A routing model books every farm before its sowing date; hired crews fill any gap.
  4. Store and deliver from five depots, month by month.

Industry

  1. Sign a volume for the months you need: CBG, biomass power, boilers, pellets.
  2. Get a price built from real costs: farm-gate, collection, storage, haulage, margin.
  3. Receive straw all year, not just in a six-week rush, from fields that were cleared, not burned.

The satellite record NASA FIRMS via Google Earth Engine

Fewer fires are counted. The burned area hasn't moved.

Satellite fire detections in Punjab and Haryana each paddy season (Sep–Dec), counted day by day inside official district boundaries. Counts have fallen since 2021 under enforcement, but the area burned in 2024 matched 2023, which suggests burning moved to times the satellites miss. So we use fires as a signal of when straw comes off the fields, and take how much from crop statistics.

Selling straw nets a farmer roughly nothing, and a baler that arrives after the sowing date is worthless. We take the whole job off the farmer: free clearing before sowing and a small payment the same day. We make it pay by planning collection better than anyone else and selling the straw year-round.

Who needs this straw

Punjab's industry already uses millions of tonnes a year

The state put use off the field (power plants, biogas, boilers, pellets) at 5.96 Mt in 2024 and targets about 7 Mt in 2025. What industry lacks is reliable, year-round supply.

What's real and what's simulated

  • Satellite fire history (25 seasons), the TimesFM forecast and its backtests
  • District straw from government crop statistics; industry demand from published figures
  • The Gemini agent, voice in Punjabi, Hindi and English, photo grading
  • The optimizers: posted prices, crew routes, deliveries
  • Farms, customers, crews and partner crews are demo data
  • Payments are simulated; cost figures marked “assumption” are our working estimates
